Methodology

KRI-05Access reviews completed on time
Share of access reviews due in the month that were completed by their due date.		lower is worseAmber 90 / Red 80Amber 90 / Red 80
KRI-06Phishing simulation click rate
Clicks divided by simulated phishing emails sent in the month.		higher is worseAmber 8 / Red 12Amber 8 / Red 12

Enterprise ratios are pooled (total numerator over total denominator), not an average of business-unit percentages, so large units carry proportionate weight. A value exactly on a threshold takes the worse status.
